# Final training curve contract

Question: does the additional native 1080p training improve reconstruction at the same actual transmitted byte counts?

Evidence: all 60 measurements from five checkpoint stages, three fixed same-scene temporal holdouts and four token budgets. The curve shows the arithmetic mean of the three per-video PSNR values at each budget; the x coordinate is the mean actual byte count. Every within-video checkpoint comparison has exactly matched bytes. All individual records remain in final_summary.csv; no observations or negative changes are removed.

Archetype: one quantitative comparison panel, with the final checkpoint emphasized. Backend: the established Python/Matplotlib workflow. No seed uncertainty, confidence interval, significance test, convergence or cross-video generalization claim is made.

Export: 180 x 105 mm; editable PDF/SVG and 600 dpi PNG. The minimum text size is 7 pt. One panel makes panel alignment not applicable; preserve the alignment record, source preflight, PDF glyph audit, collision audit and a rendered visual check. This is a research report figure, not a journal submission compliance claim.
